Guiding Purpose

OUR MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To proclaim the life-transforming gospel of Jesus Christ, disciple believers in sound biblical doctrine, minister in the power of the Holy Spirit, and serve our communities with unconditional Christian love and compassionate service.

Our Vision

To be an impactful, reproducing church in Andhra Pradesh and beyond, where every generation is ignited with a passion for God's presence, grounded in His Word, and empowered to shine as lights in the world.

Spiritual Heritage

Pastor N. Michael Paul continues a distinguished generational ministry lineage as the second son of Dr. N. Jayapaul, honoring a legacy of decades of pioneering evangelism, mass crusades, and deep love for God's kingdom.

What We Believe

STATEMENT OF FAITH

Core biblical truths that define our doctrine, our preaching, and our Christian walk.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God, providing complete guidance for faith, doctrine, and daily Christian living.

The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three distinct Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, equal in power, majesty, and glory.

Salvation by Grace

We believe that salvation is a free gift of God's grace, received solely through personal faith in Jesus Christ, His atoning death, and bodily resurrection.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the active presence, indwelling, and empowering of the Holy Spirit for holy living, spiritual gifts, and bold witness.

The Ordinances

We observe Water Baptism by immersion and regular Holy Communion in remembrance of Christ's broken body and shed blood.

The Blessed Hope

We believe in the personal, imminent, and glorious return of our Lord Jesus Christ, our blessed hope and anchor for eternity.
