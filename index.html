Our Firm Foundation

BUILT ON THE ROCK

In The Calvary Church Vijayawada, we stand firmly built upon the unshakeable foundation of faith in Jesus Christ. Under the faithful leadership and spiritual oversight of Pastor N Michael Paul and Sis. Sami Symphony Paul, our church family is rooted in the truth of God's Word.

"For no other foundation can anyone lay than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ." — 1 Corinthians 3:11. With deep devotion and sound teaching, we continue to grow together as a family of believers, established in faith and abounding in thanksgiving.

Sacred Ordinance

HOLY COMMUNION SERVICE

Every First Sunday we do have a Holy Communion Sunday in remembrance of His Sacrifice.

The Table of Remembrance

The bread and cup prepared in reverence, keeping the remembrance of His sacrifice at the heart of our service.

Prayer & Thanksgiving

Pastor N Michael Paul leading the congregation in prayer, sanctifying the elements in a spirit of gratitude.

Serving the Communion

Serving the elements to the church family in unity and Christian fellowship.

The Bread of Life

"Take, eat; this is My body which is broken for you; do this in remembrance of Me." — 1 Corinthians 11:24

The New Covenant

"This cup is the new covenant in My blood. This do, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of Me." — 1 Corinthians 11:25

The Blood of the Covenant

Symbolizing the cleansing blood of Jesus Christ, poured out for many for the remission of sins.
