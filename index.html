Sacred Ordinance

HOLY COMMUNION SERVICE

Every First Sunday of the month, we partake in Holy Communion in remembrance of Christ's sacrifice.

The Table of Remembrance

The bread and cup prepared in reverence, keeping the remembrance of His sacrifice at the center of worship.

Prayer & Thanksgiving

Pastor N. Michael Paul sanctifying the elements in prayer and leading the congregation in thanksgiving.

Serving the Elements

Serving the body and blood of Christ to believers in unity, love, and sacred fellowship.

The Bread of Life

"Take, eat; this is My body which is broken for you; do this in remembrance of Me." — 1 Corinthians 11:24

The New Covenant

"This cup is the new covenant in My blood. This do, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of Me." — 1 Corinthians 11:25

The Cleansing Blood

Symbolizing the precious blood of Jesus Christ, poured out for many for the remission of sins.
