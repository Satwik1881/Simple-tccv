OUR CHURCH MINISTRIES

Engage with our diverse ministry teams dedicated to spiritual growth, Christ-centered community, and serving our cities.

Home Ministries

Worship & Music

Directed by Sis. Sami Symphony Paul

"Serve the Lord with gladness; come before His presence with singing." — Psalm 100:2

Bringing our congregation into the presence of God through spirit-filled Telugu Christian hymns, contemporary worship, and choral harmony. Our worship teams minister weekly in both Vijayawada and Guntur.

Rehearsals Saturdays | Services Sundays

Women's Fellowship

Led by Sis. Sami Symphony Paul

"Charm is deceitful and beauty is passing, but a woman who fears the Lord, she shall be praised." — Proverbs 31:30

A nurturing space where sisters in Christ study God's Word, intercede for their families, and receive practical biblical wisdom on marriage, motherhood, and Christian living.

Bi-Weekly Prayer Gatherings

Youth & NextGen

Pastoral Guidance

"Remember now your Creator in the days of your youth." — Ecclesiastes 12:1

Igniting young people with a holy passion for God. Through candid Q&A sessions, contemporary worship, and mentorship, we equip youth to stand firm against worldly culture.

Monthly Youth Rallies

Sunday School

Dedicated Teachers

"Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of heaven." — Matthew 19:14

Age-appropriate biblical teaching for toddlers, children, and pre-teens. Children learn foundational scripture verses, Christian songs, and moral values through loving mentors.

Every Sunday at 10:00 AM (Vijayawada)

Prayer & Intercession

Prayer Warriors

"Pray without ceasing, in everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you." — 1 Thess 5:17-18

The spiritual engine of the church. Faithful intercessors who lift up every prayer request received via our website, praying over the sick, brokenhearted, and our nation.

Continuous Intercessory Chain

Community Outreach

Compassion Team

"Assuredly, I say to you, inasmuch as you did it to one of the least of these My brethren, you did it to Me." — Matthew 25:40

Serving the needy, visiting the hospitalized, and extending the tangible love of Christ through charity, prayer visits, and community support in Vijayawada and Guntur.

Monthly Outreach Missions
