Lead Pastor

Pastor N. Michael Paul

Pastor N. Michael Paul is the Senior and Founding Pastor of The Calvary Church Vijayawada – Guntur. He is the second son of the legendary evangelist Dr. N. Jayapaul. Raised in an atmosphere of prayer, evangelism, and intense devotion to the Gospel, Pastor Michael heard God's clear call upon his life to preach the sound doctrine of grace and truth.

Known for his clear, exegetical, and spirit-anointed preaching, Pastor Michael shepherds thousands of believers across both the Vijayawada congregation (at Swarnas Convention) and the Guntur congregation (at Vidyanagar). His messages consistently focus on spiritual renewal, personal holiness, knowing God's will, and walking in unconditional faith.

Beyond the local pulpit, Pastor Michael reaches a global audience through his digital broadcasts, fasting prayer series, and social media platforms, touching lives with the healing and transformative love of Jesus Christ.

Co-Pastor & Worship Leader

Sis. Sami Symphony Paul

Sis. Sami Symphony Paul ministers alongside Pastor N. Michael Paul with deep passion, musical excellence, and spiritual discernment. As the principal worship leader of The Calvary Church, her ministry creates an atmosphere where hearts are humbled and uplifted in true worship.

She actively directs early dawn services in Guntur, oversees the Women's Ministry, and leads the church's vocal and instrumental teams. Her compassionate pastoral heart has made her a trusted counselor, mentor to young women, and prayer intercessor for families across both cities.
